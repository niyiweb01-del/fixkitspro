Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

FIX KIT sells digital tools for online stores. This policy explains what we collect when you browse the site, buy a product, or contact us.

What we collect

  • Name and email address at checkout (required to process payment and deliver downloads)
  • Payment details processed by Paystack when you check out
  • Product, amount, and payment reference for order fulfillment
  • Messages you send through contact or newsletter forms
  • Basic technical logs needed to run the site and prevent abuse

How we use it

  • To process payments and deliver downloads or manual fulfillments to the email you provide
  • To send receipts and support replies
  • To improve the storefront and fix issues

Payments

Card and bank payments are handled by Paystack. We do not store full card numbers on FIX KIT servers. See Paystack’s own privacy documentation for how they process payment data.

Downloads

Instant products are delivered through a secure link tied to your verified payment reference. Keep that reference private — anyone with it and a valid download token can retrieve the file.

Contact

Questions about privacy or your data: use the contact form on the homepage.
